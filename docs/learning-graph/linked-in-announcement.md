## Final Version

Thrilled to share a new open educational resource: Introduction to 3D Printing - a free, AI-assisted level 2.9 interactive intelligent textbook for high school students in CTE, STEM, and pre-engineering pathways!

This course context was derived by analyzing four core sources: PLTW engineering pedagogy, ASTM/ISO 52900 additive manufacturing standards, America Makes workforce competencies, and explicit alignment with community-college articulation pathways added by analyzing topics covered by Hennepin Technical College, DCTC, Dunwoody, and the broader Minnesota State system.  The content was then enriched with new advanced topics such as the use of AI/ML to analyze streaming video of the printing process for quickly detecting printing errors.



Students learn the full AM workflow: parametric CAD, mesh files, slicing, FDM and resin printer operation, materials science, DfAM principles, metrology, troubleshooting, post-processing, safety, and career pathways — concluding with a capstone engineering-design project portfolio.

• Detailed 292 glossary terms with example usage

• 81 FAQs to train your chatbot answers

• Detailed annotated references for each chapter

• ~92,000 words (~380 equivalent printed pages)
