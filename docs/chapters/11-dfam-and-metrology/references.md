# References: Design for Additive Manufacturing and Metrology

1. [Design for additive manufacturing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Design_for_additive_manufacturing) - Wikipedia - Overview of DfAM principles including overhang angle limits, support minimization strategies, build orientation decision-making, and topology optimization for AM.

2. [Geometric dimensioning and tolerancing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometric_dimensioning_and_tolerancing) - Wikipedia - Comprehensive article on GD&T symbols, datum reference frames, feature control frames, and tolerance interpretation used to specify and inspect printed part accuracy.

3. [Metrology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metrology) - Wikipedia - Overview of measurement science including instruments, calibration, traceability, and uncertainty — the scientific foundation for evaluating whether a printed part meets specifications.

4. Design for Additive Manufacturing — David W. Rosen — ASME Press — Systematic DfAM framework covering build orientation strategy, support minimization, lattice design, tolerance allowances, and topology optimization for functional AM parts.

5. Fundamentals of Dimensional Metrology (6th ed.) — Connie Dotson — Cengage — Covers calipers, micrometers, dial indicators, surface plates, and GD&T interpretation — the measurement tools and methods used to evaluate printed part dimensional accuracy.

6. [Selecting the right 3D printing process](https://www.hubs.com/knowledge-base/selecting-right-3d-printing-process/) - Hubs (Protolabs Network) - Embeds DfAM decision-making into process selection, showing how orientation and support choices directly affect achievable accuracy, surface finish, and cost.

7. [What is FDM 3D printing?](https://www.hubs.com/knowledge-base/what-is-fdm-3d-printing/) - Hubs (Protolabs Network) - FDM-specific DfAM guidance covering overhang angle limits, minimum wall thickness, bridging distance, and recommended tolerance allowances for functional parts.

8. [3D printer calibration guide](https://teachingtechyt.github.io/calibration.html) - Teaching Tech - Includes dimensional accuracy testing with calibration cubes and flow rate calibration exercises directly applicable to metrology practice in this chapter.

9. [Print quality troubleshooting](https://www.simplify3d.com/resources/print-quality-troubleshooting/) - Simplify3D - Connects DfAM decisions (orientation, support placement) to measurable print quality outcomes including dimensional accuracy, surface roughness, and warping.

10. [Printables](https://www.printables.com) - Prusa Research - Community repository of calibration objects (cubes, tolerance towers, flow benchmarks, Benchy) used to develop and practice the metrology skills covered in this chapter.
