# References: Digital Workflows, Careers, Articulation, and the Capstone Project

1. [Manufacturing engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manufacturing_engineering) - Wikipedia - Overview of the manufacturing engineering profession covering career pathways, educational requirements, industry sectors, and the role of additive manufacturing in modern production.

2. [Articulation (education)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Articulation_(education)) - Wikipedia - Explains credit articulation agreements between high schools and community colleges, how CTE courses qualify for dual credit, and how articulation transcripts support college admissions.

3. [Version control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Version_control) - Wikipedia - Covers version control systems and workflows applicable to managing CAD file revisions, slicer profiles, and engineering design documentation across a capstone project lifecycle.

4. Engineering Portfolio Guide — Project Lead The Way (PLTW) — PLTW Publications — Official guidance on building engineering portfolios for college applications and articulated-credit submissions, covering documentation standards and presentation rubrics.

5. Wohlers Report 2023 — Terry Wohlers et al. — Wohlers Associates — Career landscape overview documenting AM workforce demand, job categories, in-demand skills, and the certification pathways (SME, America Makes, NC3) connecting education to employment.

6. [Project Lead The Way](https://pltw.org) - PLTW - Official PLTW engineering program resources including capstone project documentation templates, design challenge rubrics, and links to college articulation agreements nationwide.

7. [NCCER](https://nccer.org) - National Center for Construction Education & Research - Workforce credentials and apprenticeship programs connecting CTE completers to manufacturing career pathways through nationally recognized certifications and employer partnerships.

8. [Hubs Knowledge Base](https://www.hubs.com/knowledge-base/) - Hubs (Protolabs Network) - Professional digital manufacturing workflow reference covering file naming conventions, version management, and production queue management applicable to capstone project workflows.

9. [Printables](https://www.printables.com) - Prusa Research - Platform demonstrating professional maker workflows including model versioning, public documentation, and community presentation — skills directly applicable to capstone portfolio development.

10. [Onshape Education](https://www.onshape.com/en/education) - Onshape - Cloud CAD platform with built-in version control, collaborative design review, and portfolio export features directly applicable to capstone project documentation and articulated-credit submission.
